Egg Roll

Indian-style street-food egg roll

An egg roll is a popular Indian street-food snack made by wrapping a flavorful egg filling inside a soft, lightly crispy roti or paratha. The egg is cooked directly onto the flatbread, creating a delicious layer that is seasoned with salt, pepper, and spices. It is usually filled with thinly sliced onions, green chillies, fresh coriander, and a splash of lemon juice, then topped with green chutney, ketchup, or chilli sauce. The combination of the warm egg, flaky paratha, crunchy onions, and tangy sauces makes it a satisfying, flavorful, and convenient meal.

Ingredients

For the rote/paratha

  • 2 medium maida rotis or parathas
  • 1-2 tsp oil

Step

  1. Heat a tawa over medium heat.
  2. Place the roti or paratha on the tawa
  3. Cook until it is lightly golden and almost done
  4. Remove it and keep it aside temporarily

For the egg filling

  • 2 eggs
  • salt, to taste
  • black pepper, to taste
  • 1/4 tsp red chilli powder
  • 1-2 tsp oil

step

  1. Crack eggs into a bowl.
  2. Add salt, black pepper, and optional red chill powder.
  3. Beat the egg well.
  4. Heat a little oil on the tawa.
  5. Pour the eggs onto the tawa and spread it slightly.
  6. Immediately place the roti over the egg.
  7. Press gently so the egg sticks to the roti
  8. Cook until the egg is fully set.

for the onion filling

  • 1 medium onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 green chilli, finely chopped
  • fresh coriander
  • 1/2 tsp lemon juice
  • pinch of chaat masala

step

  1. thinly slice on onion.
  2. Add chopped green chilli and fresh coriander.
  3. Add a little lemon juice.
  4. Sprinkle chaat masala if desired.
  5. Mix everything together.

For serving

  • Green chuntney
  • Tomato ketchup or chilli sauce

step

  1. Place the egg-covered roti on a plate.
  2. Add the onion filling in the center.
  3. Drizzle green chutney, ketchup, or chilli sauce.
  4. Add a little butter or extra chaat masala if desired.
  5. Roll it tightly and wrap the bottom with butter paper or foil.
  6. Serve hot.
