Palak Paneer

Palak Paneer: Description

Palak Paneer is a popular Indian vegetarian dish made with soft paneer cubes cooked in a smooth, creamy spinach gravy. The spinach is blended with aromatic spices and lightly seasoned to create a rich, nutritious, and flavorful curry that is commonly served with roti, naan, or rice.

Ingredients

  • 250 g paneer, cubed
  • 250–300 g fresh spinach (palak)
  • 1 medium onion, chopped
  • 2 medium tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1–2 green chillies
  • 1–2 tbsp oil or ghee
  • ½ tsp cumin seeds
  • ¼ tsp turmeric powder
  • ½ tsp coriander powder
  • ½ tsp garam masala
  • 2–3 tbsp cream (optional)
  • Salt, to taste
  • Water
  • Fresh coriander (optional)

step

  1. Wash the spinach thoroughly.
  2. Blanch the spinach in boiling water for 2–3 minutes.
  3. Transfer it to cold water to retain its green color.
  4. Blend spinach with green chillies into a smooth puree.
  5. Heat oil in a pan and add cumin seeds.
  6. Add chopped onions and sauté until lightly golden.
  7. Add ginger-garlic paste and cook for a minute.
  8. Add chopped tomatoes, turmeric, coriander powder, and salt.
  9. Cook until the tomatoes soften and the masala is well cooked.
  10. Add the spinach puree and simmer for 5–7 minutes.
  11. Add paneer cubes and gently mix.
  12. Add garam masala and optional cream.
  13. Simmer for another 2–3 minutes without overcooking the spinach.
  14. Serve hot with roti, naan, or rice.
